# CORE SCIENTIFIC INTERPRETATION

Phase 3 proved that target-specific local surface features matter.

Phase 4 showed that broad aggregation of many independent surface votes provides only modest additional benefit.

> Different anatomical targets require different, structured external surface support regions, and a specialized residual refiner can extract local geometric relationships that a global query decoder cannot resolve.

The refiner must NOT attempt to predict an organ from scratch.

It receives a coarse prediction and predicts only a bounded correction.

# STAGE 3

# TARGET-SPECIFIC SURFACE SUPPORT SET

For every target \(k\), construct a support set:

$$
S_k = \{j_1,\ldots,j_M\}
$$

from external surface tokens only.

The support set should combine evidence already learned in Phases 3 and 4.

Candidate scores may include:

$$
s_{jk}
=
\lambda_a \alpha_{jk}
+
\lambda_v w_{jk}
$$

where:

* \(\alpha_{jk}\) = target-query surface attention
* \(w_{jk}\) = voting confidence

Do not tune many arbitrary weights.

Initial simple option:

$$
s_{jk}
=
\frac{1}{2}
\left(
\tilde\alpha_{jk}
+
\tilde w_{jk}
\right)
$$

after suitable normalization.

# STAGE 4

# DO NOT USE AN INTERNAL SPHERE

Do NOT define a refinement ROI simply as:

$$
\|x_j-p_k^0\| < 50\text{ mm}
$$

Many internal torso targets are much farther than 50 mm from the skin.

Instead use externally meaningful support selection.

Permitted support mechanisms include:

### A. Attention-selected points

Top-M by target-query attention.

### B. Vote-selected points

Top-M by voting confidence.

### C. Combined attention/vote support

Union or fused ranking.

### D. Anatomical axial band

Surface points near the coarse target's superior/inferior coordinate:

$$
|z_j-z_k^0| < h_k
$$

where \(h_k\) is chosen from TRAIN data or via INNER_DEV.

### E. Multiple connected surface neighborhoods

Take top scoring anchor surface tokens and expand around them using Euclidean/geodesic neighborhoods on the external surface.

None may use the ground-truth target.

# STAGE 5

# MULTI-ANCHOR SUPPORT

Do not assume one external patch supports each organ.

For each target, identify the top:

```text
K_anchor = 2–4
```

high-scoring surface anchors, subject to minimum spatial separation.

For example:

1. select highest-score token;
2. suppress tokens within a chosen external-surface radius;

4. repeat.

This allows a target to use multiple views such as:

```text

posterior surface
left lateral surface
right lateral surface
```

when useful.

Choose anchor count on INNER_DEV.

# STAGE 12

# TARGET-CONDITIONED POOLING

Do not simply max-pool the support region.

Use the target query to pool relevant local evidence.

Conceptually:

$$

=

$$

where \(h_{jk}\) is the locally refined support-token representation.

This is a second-stage, target-specific evidence aggregation.

# STAGE 13

# BOUNDED RESIDUAL

The refiner must predict a correction, not a new unrestricted coordinate.

Use:

$$
\Delta p_k
=

\tanh(z_k)
$$

Determine \(R_k\) using TRAINING data.

For each target calculate coarse Phase 4 residual magnitude:

$$
e_{ik}^{0}
=
\|p_{ik}-p_{ik}^{0}\|
$$

Use a robust training-only statistic such as:

```text
P95 or P99 of coarse residual magnitude
```

to define an appropriate bound.

You may use:

* one global R
* anatomical-group R
* target-specific R

Compare simplicity and stability.

Do NOT use validation residuals to choose R.

# STAGE 14

# CORRECTION MAGNITUDE REGULARIZATION

$$

=

$$
