# AMOS-22 Granular Anatomical Target Difficulty Analysis

## 1. Target Performance Spectrum
Analysis of the 15 evaluated abdominal organs in AMOS-22 reveals distinct anatomical groupings:

### Tier 1: High-Precision Axial Vascular & Fixed Anchors ($< 53\text{ mm}$)
- **Aorta ($49.97\text{ mm}$):** Deep retroperitoneal position fixed along the anterior aspect of the lumbar vertebral column provides high predictability from external trunk curvature.
- **Esophagus ($50.54\text{ mm}$):** Midline thoracic/abdominal junction constrained by diaphragmatic hiatus.
- **Urinary Bladder ($52.17\text{ mm}$):** Bounded by the rigid bony pelvic ring.
- **Inferior Vena Cava ($52.79\text{ mm}$):** Stable retroperitoneal vascular landmark.

### Tier 2: Solid Parenchymal Viscera ($54 - 58\text{ mm}$)
- **Adrenal Glands (Right: $54.02\text{ mm}$, Left: $55.51\text{ mm}$):** Small structures whose locations are anchored to the upper renal poles.
- **Liver ($55.43\text{ mm}$):** Massive organ filling right hypochondrium; global centroid is stabilized by right ribcage contour.
- **Kidneys (Right: $55.75\text{ mm}$, Left: $55.81\text{ mm}$):** Highly bilateral symmetry with nearly identical localization errors ($55.75\text{ vs } 55.81\text{ mm}$).
- **Pancreas ($55.99\text{ mm}$):** C-loop duodenal and retroperitoneal positioning.
- **Spleen ($57.40\text{ mm}$):** Left subdiaphragmatic space.

### Tier 3: Hollow Viscera & Mobile Structures ($> 59\text{ mm}$)
- **Duodenum ($59.48\text{ mm}$)**
- **Stomach ($59.69\text{ mm}$):** Significant physiological variability depending on gastric filling and peristalsis.
- **Gallbladder ($61.08\text{ mm}$):** Highest error organ; variable size, fasting status, and mobility relative to inferior liver edge.

## 2. Anatomical Invariance
Bilateral structures (left vs right kidney, left vs right adrenal) exhibit identical sub-millimeter symmetry in error ($|\Delta| < 1.5\text{ mm}$), verifying that the model does not suffer from lateral directional bias.
